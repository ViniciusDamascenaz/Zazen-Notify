# Zazen

> **Esse momento nunca mais vai existir.**

Zazen is a minimalist Android application designed to bring you back to the present moment.

In a world constantly competing for our attention through notifications, messages, social media and endless information, Zazen takes a different approach: it doesn't ask you to disconnect from the world.

It simply reminds you to **notice the moment you're already in**.

## The Concept

Zazen was built around a simple idea:

**You don't need to escape your life to be present in it. You need to learn how to return to it.**

The application periodically delivers short prompts designed to interrupt autopilot and bring your attention back to what is happening right now.

You might be asked to notice your breathing, feel your body, pay attention to a sound around you, observe something in your environment or simply recognize what you are experiencing in that exact moment.

These aren't traditional motivational quotes.

They are small invitations to observe.

And that's the important part.

Zazen isn't meant to make you dependent on notifications to be present.

The goal is almost the opposite.

Each reminder is an opportunity to practice returning to the present. With time, the hope is that this becomes less dependent on the application — and more natural to the person using it.

You receive a reminder.

You return to the present.

You practice.

Eventually, you learn to return on your own.

## Philosophy

Modern technology is extremely good at taking our attention.

Notifications, messages, social media, videos, news and countless other stimuli constantly give us reasons to look somewhere else.

But the present moment doesn't need to compete for our attention.

It is already happening.

The philosophy behind Zazen is not about rejecting technology or trying to permanently disconnect from the modern world.

It is about changing the relationship we have with our attention.

Technology can be used to distract us.

But it can also be used to remind us to stop, even if only for a few seconds, and notice that we are here.

That's what Zazen tries to do.

Not to give you another thing to consume.

Not to give you another feed to scroll.

Not to tell you how you should feel.

Just to bring you back.

Because every moment you experience is temporary.

**This exact moment will never exist again.**

## Features

* Minimalist interface
* Customizable notification schedule
* Randomized mindfulness prompts
* Non-repeating prompts
* Sensory and breathing exercises
* Lightweight Android application
* Designed to work quietly in the background

## Development

Zazen was developed while I was beginning my studies with **Android Studio and Android development**.

The project became an opportunity to learn by building something real, from the Android application structure to notifications, background scheduling, permissions, user preferences and the different components required to make the application work reliably.

Rather than building a tutorial project, I decided to use the learning process to create something that I genuinely wanted to exist.

Zazen is therefore both a personal software project and a practical part of my journey into Android development.
